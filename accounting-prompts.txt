AI/Accounting (QBO replacement)
Goal is to create a superior executive management suite of tools to better inform business operations as we evolve into the future
The paradigm of our current FM structure will need work. 
AI possibilities around reporting that can be created with the integration of AI into the professional accounting space.

Make copy of our current FM, to create a new family of layouts and integration schemes. 

current bookkeeping approach
	we control/reconcile the all economic activity to the Ticket as the central unifying economic entity. 

proposed approach
	reconcile all the economic activity to the Contract as the central locus, 
	removing all payment-related information from the Ticket, 
	which along with payments and other subset domains would subordinate to the Contract.
	The balance would be controlled on one master contract ledger
		chronologically by Date Processed 
		contains all charges and payments processed 
		maintains a running total with a minimum of complexity. 
		All postings of charges and payments would be posted to this contract ledger.
	The ROS would be KDA-aligned and would have its own ledger. 
	All Sales and Payment events would be a function of the contract. 
	rename Ticket Charges as Sales, 
	rename Ticket as Work Order, 
	and would operate Work Orders
		as a primary subgrouping under Contracts 
		in parallel with Sales and Payment
		Work Order status would be pending or processed.
	Sales and Payments would net to a rolling total balance owed on the Contract.
	rename “Contract" as Service Agreement.


This also will ultimately solve the frustration we’ve had with...
	Eaches 
	and contractually complex single customer, commercial and industrial accounts, 
	with an all-work-orders-created-equal approach.

HawksBase strictly mimicked our manual bookkeeping process, 
	which controlled to the work ticket, 
	organized by date and technician on a daily report 
	that also controlled collections of cash and checks.
